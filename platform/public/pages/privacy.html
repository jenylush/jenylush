Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026

What we collect

Couples: your name, email address, password (stored as a secure hash) and the content you add to your website. Guests: the details a couple adds to their guest list and the answers guests give in RSVP forms, such as attendance, plus-one names and dietary or accessibility needs.

How we use it

To run your website, deliver RSVPs to you, and send the emails you ask us to send (for example invitations, RSVP confirmations and reminders). Guests only receive optional wedding updates if they opt in, and every email includes a link to manage preferences.

Analytics

We record anonymous product events (for example “website published”) linked to account IDs, without names, email addresses or RSVP answers, to understand what helps couples. Website visit counts are aggregate numbers.

Photos

Uploaded photos are processed to remove hidden metadata such as GPS location. Photos are private until they appear on a published website.

Sharing

We use an email delivery provider to send emails and a hosting provider to store data. We do not sell personal data.

Your choices

Download all of your data or delete your account from your account settings. Guests can unsubscribe from a couple's emails using the link in any email, or ask the couple to remove them from the guest list.

Contact

Email hello@example.com with any privacy question.
