FAQ

Questions couples ask

Do I need any design or technical skills?

Not at all. Pick a template, fill in a few details and upload your photos. Everything is point-and-click, and you can see exactly what guests will see before you publish.

Is it really free?

Yes. Creating, publishing and sharing your website, collecting RSVPs and exporting your guest list are free. Premium is optional and adds extra templates, design controls and scheduled emails.

Can I change my template later?

Any time. Your words, photos and guest list are stored separately from the design, so switching templates never loses anything.

What address will my website have?

You choose it — for example amara-and-theo. We check it is available before it is yours. Premium couples will be able to connect their own domain.

Can I keep my website private?

Your website is hidden from search engines unless you choose otherwise. You can also make it private while you work on it, or protect it with a password on Premium.

How do guests RSVP?

Guests fill in a simple form on your website. You can send personal invitation links so their details are filled in for them, and they can update their answer later — you always see their latest response.

Will you email my guests without asking?

Never. Emails only go out when you send invitations or turn on a reminder, and guests only receive updates if they opt in. Every email includes a way to unsubscribe.

Can I download my data?

Yes — export your guest list as a spreadsheet at any time, or download everything from your account settings. You can delete your account and data whenever you like.
