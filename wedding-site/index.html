Our story

From Shuttlers Paddi to Forever

Every love story has two sides. Open each one to read how it happened, in our own words.

I.Where it began

“Do you live around here?”

He was a brand designer at my company, while I was in the tech department. A few days after he resumed, we happened to get on the same Shuttlers bus after work.

He got down at the same junction where I usually got down, and the next workday, he came over and asked if I lived around there. I said yes. That simple conversation was the beginning of our friendship.

II.Just friends, for a whole year

Shuttlers paddi

He stayed at the company for just about two months, but we sat close to each other on the Shuttlers after work a few times before he left. By then we were Shuttlers paddi, and even after he resigned, the friendship continued.

For the entire first year, we were genuinely just friends — two friends talking about work, church, family and life. He became someone I could count on, in the good times and the not-so-good ones.

III.A movie & two weeks of silence

The plot twist

About a year into our friendship, we disagreed over a movie. We both had very strong and very different opinions, and somehow that movie earned us almost two weeks of silence.

Two weeks without talking to him felt way too long. That was when I realised I actually liked him. As it turns out, he had come to the same realisation.

IV.September 2025

He asked me out

Eventually, he reached out, we made up, and we went back to being us. Only this time, things were a little different: we became more intentional about each other.

In September 2025, he asked me out. And I said YES.

V.Seven months later

The proposal

Seven months later, he proposed. And now, the man who started out as my colleague, became my friend, became my Shuttlers paddi, and eventually became the person I couldn’t imagine my life without… is going to be my husband.

Tadaaaaa, here we are!
